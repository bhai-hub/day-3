ANATOMICAL SIZING FOR CLINICAL PRACTICE

When performing microsurgery or clinical procedures, changes in hand temperature and glove compression can alter ring snugness.

  1. 1. Measure at Rest: Wrap a flexible measuring strip around the proximal base of the finger at standard room temperature.
  2. 2. Account for Knuckle Clearance: Ensure the measured circumference can slide comfortably across the proximal interphalangeal joint.
  3. 3. Glove Comfort: If you frequently wear size 6.5–7.0 surgical gloves, choose a ring size calibrated with a 0.25mm relaxed clearance.
Complimentary custom sizing rings and anatomical gauges can be dispatched directly to your clinic or hospital upon request.

What does the Design of the Draft represent?

ELIMINATING COGNITIVE LOAD

An interface stripped of clutter, aggressive colors, and unnecessary pop ups or text immediately lowers cognitive load, turning the experience of the customer beautiful.

MIRRORING & BELONGING

Opening directly onto a dignified portrait of a physician creates instant, silent recognition. Without a single word of marketing, the visitor feels warmth, fostering deep trust into the community.

RESPECT FOR HIGH INTELLIGENCE

Elite medical practitioners dislike being "sold to" or over-explained. Ultra-minimalism respects their discernment, letting the purity of the materials, lighting, and craftsmanship speak quietly without persuasion tactics.

THE ACTUAL FEEL OF LUXURY
